Common questions

Where is my progress saved?
On your phone. There is no account, so we cannot restore a save for you. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes your save, unless your phone's Android backup has kept a copy.

Why can't I make an offering at the altar?
The altar only offers when an ad is available. If none is, your game carries on normally and you lose nothing.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?
Where these choices apply in your region, open the game's settings and tap Privacy options. The privacy policy has more detail.
