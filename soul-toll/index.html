Soul Toll

A gothic idle game for Android. Coming soon to Google Play.

Pull the rope and the bell tolls across the graveyard. Souls rise from the graves and drift to it, and they keep gathering while you are away.

Spend them on upgrades that deepen the graveyard, then consecrate the ground to begin again stronger.

  • No account and no sign-in
  • No purchases
  • One optional ad, only when you choose to make an offering at the altar
